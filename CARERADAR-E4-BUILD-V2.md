## 1. Page-level pacing

E4 is not a section that happens to be illustrated. It is the moment the page has been building toward, and the sections around it are staged to make that arrival feel earned.

| Block | Role | Visual register |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Opening statement | Pure typography. Paper texture on the ground plane, barely perceptible. No figures, no motion. |
| E2 | Recognition of the situation | Still typographic. A single paper edge — a fold line with its shadow — enters at the section boundary. First hint that the page is made of something. |
| E3 | The fork | The fork pictogram is the page's **first illustrated object**, sanctioned by the block's own design note. Paper-cut, bold outline, static. It reads as a promise. |
| **E4** | **The performance** | **Full paper-cut motion. Seven scenes.** |
| E5–E8 | Explanation, consolidation | Paper language recedes to flat cards and comparison layouts. Material vocabulary retained, motion dropped. |
| E10–E14 | Stance, capacity, close | Fully quiet. Typography and calm iconography only. |

**The gradient:** texture → edge → object → motion → object → edge → texture. The page opens flat, comes alive, and settles flat again. A visitor who skims still feels the arc.

**Practical constraint on E3.** The fork pictogram must be drawn in the same hand as the E4 scenes and must be the *only* illustrated element before E4. If a second appears — an icon in E2, a decorative mark in E1 — the arrival at E4 loses its charge.

## 2. Material system

Continuity comes from the material, not from any persistent object. Documents appear, do their work, and leave. New ones emerge as previous ones complete their role.

### 2.1 Construction

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Outline | Bold black, ~3px at 1x, hand-varied — never uniform weight |
| Edges | Scissor-cut wobble, 1–2px deviation from true. Imperfection is the point |
| Shadow | Soft, offset 4px/6px, ~12% opacity, warm-grey |
| Texture | Paper grain over fills at 5%, faint fibre flecks |
| Depth | Maximum 3 stacked layers per scene |
| Palette | CareRadar logo colours, plus paper white and outline black |
| Figures | Cut-out silhouettes, simple features, faces present, no gradients |

**Absent by decision:** glass, blur, glow, neon, gradient mesh, floating cards, depth-of-field. If it could appear on a generic SaaS page, it does not belong here.

### 2.2 The crumple cycle

Every milestone except M7 follows the same three-beat structure:

**Uncrumple.** The scene's primary document enters as a crumpled form and releases — creases straightening, facets flattening, shadows resolving. 700–900ms.

**Unfold.** Objects rise and unfold from the flattened surface, or slide in and layer over it. Staggered, 80–100ms apart, each with a short overshoot on landing.

**Crumple away.** The scene folds or crumples back as it exits. **Scrubbed to scroll position on exit, not fired on a timer** — so a visitor scrolling back up sees it uncrumple again. This is what makes the page feel like a physical process rather than a played sequence.

**The readability rule.** While a milestone occupies the viewport, its scene is fully settled and static, and its text block is complete and readable. Crumple-out begins only once the scene has passed the upper third of the viewport, and the text block does not crumple — it scrolls away normally. The illustration animates; the information never becomes unreadable.

**M7 breaks the cycle.** It uncrumples, unfolds, and stays. No crumple-away. After six repetitions the eye has learned to expect the fold-back; withholding it is the arc's resolution.

## 3. The seven milestones

The subline caveat sits above the whole timeline and must remain prominent: time figures are design values from process planning, not commitments. The Landesprüfungsamt für Gesundheitsberufe decides the procedural route and the duration of recognition case by case.

---

### M1 — Needs assessment and mandate
*Design value: two to four weeks*

#### Scene

A crumpled sheet at centre frame releases into a clean rectangle. From its surface two cut-out figures rise and fold upright, seated across a table that unfolds from the same plane. Objects layer in: a vacancy profile, an open notebook, two coffee cups, a pen laid across the paper.

The figures lean slightly toward each other. Neither presents to the other. This is a working table, not a pitch.

#### Animation

Uncrumple over 900ms, crease paths animating flat with facet shadows fading. Figures hinge upright from the sheet plane at 400ms. Objects settle 80ms apart with a short overshoot. Total 1.6s, ease-out. On exit, objects fold back into the sheet in reverse order and the sheet crumples.

#### Copy

**Before anyone is recruited, the position is defined.**

Everything downstream is built against this brief — which is why it is fixed in writing rather than discussed and remembered.

The role profile, the language level at entry, the tolerance on the procedural route, the time window and the cost frame are agreed and set down. The housing question is recorded as a clarification item. Your capacity for Praxisanleitung is captured, because it determines what is possible in the final phase.

*Lead:* CareRadar
*Your part:* Named vacancies, decision authority at the table, signature

---

### M2 — Preselection and selection decision
*Design value: four to eight weeks*

#### Scene

A crumpled form opens into a college building in flat silhouette. From its doorway, candidate cards fan out in an arc across frame — eight to ten, each carrying a simple cut-out portrait. A small seal stamps onto each corner in sequence: qualification verified at source.

Three cards lift from the arc and rotate to face the viewer, framed inside a paper video-call window with cut corners. A bold-outlined paper hand reaches in and draws one forward.

#### Animation

Cards fan on a 600ms arc, staggered 40ms. Seals stamp in sequence with a 2px settle. **The three cards lift on scroll progress rather than on a timer** — the visitor performs the selection with their own motion, which is the point of the milestone. The hand enters last and holds. On exit, cards collapse back into the fan and the fan crumples.

#### Copy

**The decision is yours, made on people we already know.**

Our contact persons at the partner colleges have watched these graduates through their entire time of study. What reaches you is not a profile forwarded by an intermediary.

The colleges put forward suitable graduates. Degree and registration are verified at source, at the Indian Nursing Council or a State Nursing Council. Structured interviews cover motivation, resilience and learning ability. You conduct selection interviews by video and name your selection in writing.

*Lead:* CareRadar with the contact persons on the ground; the decision is yours
*Your part:* Time for selection interviews, written nomination

### M3 — Contract and procedural route
*Design value: two to four weeks*

#### Scene

A crumpled sheet releases into a contract, lying flat. A pen signs across it — a hand-drawn line, deliberately imperfect. Beneath the sheet, two paper ribbons diverge and run toward the lower edge: **§16d AufenthG** and **§81a AufenthG**. The fork from E3 returns, now carrying real weight.

A small paper tag hangs from the contract corner: Tarifeinstufung.

#### Animation

Signature draws left to right over 700ms on a hand-inked path. Ribbons unroll downward as the signature completes, one slightly ahead of the other — they are alternatives, not a pair. The tag swings once and settles. On exit, ribbons roll back up and the contract crumples.

#### Copy

**Two legal routes lead to the same place.**

Which one fits depends on your timeline, and the choice is made here — with us, in writing, before anything is filed.

The employment contract or preliminary contract covers deployment as a Pflegekraft in Anerkennung, with pay regrade upon the Urkunde. Tarifeinstufung is set. The route is decided: Anerkennungspartnerschaft under §16d AufenthG, or the beschleunigtes Fachkräfteverfahren under §81a AufenthG. Power of attorney for the proceedings is granted.

In Ausbildungskohorten, the Ausbildungsvertrag and the school place confirmation take the place of the employment contract.

*Lead:* CareRadar; the contracting parties are you and the candidate
*Your part:* Tarifeinstufung, contract signature, route decision taken with us

### M4 — Qualification and official procedures
*Parallel, design value: months three to twelve*

**This is the scene the page succeeds or fails on.**

#### Scene

Three paper ribbons run **horizontally and simultaneously** across frame, braided loosely — overlapping without merging. Each carries its own objects:

- **Sprache** — speech-bubble cut-outs, alphabet cards, a level marker sliding along the ribbon
- **Anerkennungsdossier** — documents stacking one onto another, folding into a sealed envelope addressed to the Landesprüfungsamt
- **Visum** — a passport booklet opening, a stamp descending

A single bracket spans all three at the left edge, marked *Monate 3–12*.

#### Why braided

The failure mode is a visitor reading three ribbons as three consecutive steps, which would turn a twelve-to-eighteen-month route into an apparent twenty-two-month one. Braiding — strands crossing over and under — is the cheapest visual that says *at the same time* without a label carrying the load. The bracket is the safety net, not the mechanism.

#### Animation

All three strands begin moving **together**. Any stagger reintroduces the sequential reading and is not permitted. Objects populate each strand as it travels. The Sprache marker slides continuously with scroll and is the **last element still moving** after the others settle — it is the pacemaker of the overall duration, and the animation should say so before the copy does.

On exit, all three crumple together as one form.

#### Copy

**These three run at the same time.**

Which is why the phases do not simply add up — and why language progress, not paperwork, usually sets the pace.

Language training runs to the agreed level. The Anerkennungsantrag goes to the Landesprüfungsamt für Gesundheitsberufe with a complete dossier. The visa procedure runs alongside. You initiate the beschleunigtes Fachkräfteverfahren; we prepare the file completely and track every deadline.

*Lead:* CareRadar with language partners and authorities
*Your part:* Initiation under §81a AufenthG, prompt responses, readiness within the agreed window

### M5 — Departure, arrival, first day of work
*Design value: four to eight weeks, around months twelve to fourteen*

#### Scene

A crumpled form folds itself into a paper plane, which flies a dashed arc across frame over a cut-out Hamburg skyline — Elbphilharmonie, harbour cranes, church spires — in flat silhouette at three depths.

It lands and unfolds into four objects laid in a row: Anmeldung, bank card, Krankenversicherung card, Aufenthaltstitel. Behind them a facility door opens and a figure steps through.

#### Animation

The plane follows its dashed path **on scroll** — the visitor flies it. On landing it unfolds over 500ms into the four objects, settling 100ms apart. The door swings last.

Skyline layers move at three subtly different rates as the section passes. This is the only parallax on the page, justified because it depicts actual distance.

On exit, the four objects fold back into the plane and it crumples.

#### Copy

**Arrival is where most placements quietly fail.**

It is run here as a procedure, not left to someone who has just landed in a country they have never lived in.

Departure preparation and flight, pickup on arrival, Anmeldung, bank account, health insurance, Aufenthaltstitel. Your induction begins and deployment as a Pflegekraft in Anerkennung starts.

*Lead:* CareRadar for arrival and the round of authorities; you for induction and rostering
*Your part:* A clarified housing path per the mandate, induction, one named contact person in the team

### M6 — Completing recognition in ongoing operation
*Design value: six to eighteen months, alongside the job*

#### Scene

Split-level, layered in paper depth.

**Foreground:** a figure at a desk with an open examination paper and study materials — Kenntnisprüfung or Anpassungslehrgang.
**One layer back, lighter:** the same figure in the facility, working a shift alongside colleagues.

Both visible at once. A paper calendar strip runs along the base with progress marks advancing.

#### Why layered rather than side-by-side

Side-by-side reads as two options. Layered reads as two things carried simultaneously, which is the fact of this stage.

#### Animation

Background shift layer fades up first; foreground study layer slides over it. Calendar marks tick on with scroll. Nothing loops. On exit, foreground crumples first, then background — the study ends, the work continues.

#### Copy

**This stage runs on top of a full-time job.**

Which is why release time and Praxisanleitung are the two things we ask you to protect.

Either the Anpassungslehrgang at a provider approved by the Sozialbehörde, or preparation for and sitting of the Kenntnisprüfung. Practical components take place in a facility with Praxisanleitung. We track enrolments, deadlines and progress throughout.

*Lead:* You with school and authority; we remain present in the procedure
*Your part:* Release time, Praxisanleitung or a cooperation agreed for it

---

### M7 — Qualified nurse status and retention
*From the certificate onward, ongoing*

#### Scene — the resolution

The Urkunde unfolds at centre, clean and flat. Beside it a name badge changes: **Pflegefachfrau** or **Pflegefachmann** appears.

Two figures stand together — the nurse and a colleague from the team. Neither is centred; they share the frame.

**Nothing crumples.** The scene holds flat and still.

#### Animation

The Urkunde unfolds over 800ms. Badge text fades and replaces. The figures settle.

Then the page holds. This is the slowest moment on the page and should be — after six crumple-outs, the absence of one is the payoff. Do not add a closing flourish; the stillness is the effect.

#### Copy

**The certificate changes three things at once.**

The legal status, the pay grade and the roster — and it is the point from which retention, not recruitment, becomes the work.

With the Urkunde, the nurse carries the professional title Pflegefachfrau or Pflegefachmann, counts as qualified nursing staff and is regraded. The residence title changes to §18a AufenthG. We hold regular conversations with you and with the nurse and continue to support integration.

*Lead:* Joint
*Your part:* Permanent contract, reliable tariff practice, a development perspective

## 4. The Ausbildungskohorte variant

The source content states three deviations for apprenticeship cohorts. These are **not a second timeline**. They render as a narrow band beneath the seven milestones, in the flat register of E5 onward rather than the illustrated register of E4:

- Certified B2 before departure
- The Pflegeschule admission date sets the pace of entry
- The three-year apprenticeship with its state final examination replaces the Anerkennungsverfahren

Illustrating a parallel apprenticeship route would double the page's visual load for a minority path. The band does the job.

## 5. Technical specification

- **Scroll-triggered reveals throughout. No pinning.** The homepage owns the pinned mechanic.
- Entry animations fire once on enter and hold. Exit crumple is scrubbed to scroll position and reverses on upward scroll.
- Scenes are inline SVG with semantically named paths for element targeting. Rive only where a scene needs genuine multi-state behaviour — as drafted, none does.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` renders every scene's settled composition, static, immediately. All seven remain legible.
- Mobile: scenes stack full-width, simplified to two depth layers, animation reduced to fade-and-settle. **M4's braid must be tested at 360px** — if it reads as a tangle, fall back to three stacked strands with the bracket carrying the simultaneity.
- Every scene must communicate its milestone with all text hidden. Test this literally before sign-off.

---

## 6. Build order

1. **M1 as a static style frame at final quality.** Validates the paper-cut look before six more are drawn.
2. **M4 as a motion prototype.** The braid is the highest-risk element on the page; prove it early, at mobile width first.
3. Remaining five scenes.
4. E3 fork pictogram, drawn in the same hand.
5. E1/E2 texture and edge treatment.
6. Copy pass, editing and shortening — once the rhythm is visible.
